# AI Writer Lab — Business Requirements

## 1. Product Objective
Provide an AI-assisted book writing studio enabling authors to:
- Generate structured long-form content
- Improve quality iteratively
- Maintain stylistic consistency
- Export in professional publishing formats

---

## 2. Target Users
- Technical authors
- Business writers
- Analysts
- Researchers
- Enterprise knowledge teams

---

## 3. Core Value Propositions
1. Multi-agent quality improvement
2. Structured review with scoring
3. Iterative refinement loop
4. Long-form memory support
5. Multi-format publishing

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Generation
- Create project
- Generate structured outline
- Generate chapters
- Continuity across chapters

### 4.2 Review & Improvement
- Structured review scoring
- Apply improvements automatically
- Produce High Quality (orchestrated multi-agent loop)

### 4.3 Editing
- Manual chapter editing
- Regeneration

### 4.4 Export
- EPUB export
- PDF export (Cyrillic-safe)
- DOCX export

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements
- Deterministic agent protocol
- Fail-safe JSON parsing
- Bounded iteration loops
- Token-aware memory control
- Separation of concerns (generation vs export vs orchestration)

---

## 6. Strategic Positioning
AI Writer Lab is positioned as:
"An AI-powered multi-agent writing studio for long-form content production."

Not a simple text generator.
Not a chatbot.
A controlled AI production environment.

---

## 7. Long-Term Business Extensions
- SaaS deployment
- Enterprise authoring platform
- API access
- Collaboration mode
- Versioning & audit trails
